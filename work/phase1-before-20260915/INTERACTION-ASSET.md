# Phase 1 interaction sprite

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the original approved Madhur character PNG as reference. Original avatar files are retained.

Output: assets/madhur-interactions-v1.png, transparent four-pose atlas. Registration is in avatar.js.

## Prompt

Edit reference into a production sprite atlas. Preserve EXACT face, hair, beard, black shirt/trousers/shoes, brown satchel and detailed pixel-art quality of the provided Madhur game avatar. Transparent background actual alpha; no backdrop, no ground shadows, no labels or grid.
Create 4 full-body poses in ONE HORIZONTAL ROW of four equal cells. Wide landscape 2048x1024. Every character centered in its cell, identical scale and head size, feet at same baseline with generous gutters. Do not crop hair hands or feet.
Pose 1: standing front three-quarter facing screen right, both arms reaching ABOVE his head to grasp an imaginary vertical rope, elbows slightly bent, hands together near x center of body.
Pose 2: same stance and scale pulling that imaginary rope DOWN with both hands now at chest level, knees slightly flexed, shoulders working, full anatomically correct joint movement. Do NOT draw rope, it is rendered separately in the game.
Pose 3: front three-quarter, taking the cream notebook out of his brown satchel, looking down at it, both feet planted.
Pose 4: front three-quarter, holding that notebook OPEN in both hands at waist/chest level, looking toward the open pages.
Keep pose height difference from arm raising anatomically correct without rescaling the body. No added characters or mascots. Crisp faithful game pixel art, not chibi, never simplified.
